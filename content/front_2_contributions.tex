\chapter*{Author profile}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\protect\numberline{}Author profile}
Florian De Rop (1996) is a bioscience engineer. He was a researcher at the VIB/KU Leuven Stein Aerts Lab of Computational Biology between 2018 and 2023 under Stein Aerts, Suresh Poovathingal, and Jasper Janssens. His students include Koenraad De Wispelaere and Natalie Roels. He currently works at Retro Biosciences.
