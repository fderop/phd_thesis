\chapter{Conclusion and Future Perspectives}
In the very first paragraph of the very first chapter of this work, I listed some of the core questions that drive biologists. Among many other questions, we wonder how cells work internally, and how a single cell can give rise to a complex organism. I concluded that comprehensive and deterministic models for these concepts remained "elusive - almost unimaginable". In my mind, such deterministic models are no longer unimaginable today. I believe that many scientists now have clear-cut ideas of what such models will look like, and how to build them. Biological sciences are at a turning point where the combination of the vast data generation techniques that we have discussed here, combined with the latest developments in computational methods and artificial intelligence, will soon produce a complete understanding of life at the molecular scale. We find ourselves at a precipice. All we must do is dive in.
