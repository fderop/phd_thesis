\chapter*{Use of Generative AI}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\protect\numberline{}Use of Generative AI}

I did use the generative AI assistance tools Claude Sonnet 3.5, GPT-4, GPT-4.5, GPT-5, and Perplexity during the research/writing process of this thesis.

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
	\item Claude Sonnet 3.5 and GPT were used to generate all code for the new figures in this thesis, including the front page graphic.
	\item ChatGPT was used to support the literature review by helping me understand or refresh concepts.
	\item Perplexity was used as an additional search engine for up-to-date literature discovery, complementing traditional methods such as review papers, bibliographies, and databases like PubMed.
	\item GPT was used extensively for \LaTeX{} formatting of the thesis.
	\item This AI statement was entirely AI-generated.
\end{itemize}

Generative AI has only been used in accordance with the KU Leuven guidelines. I have reviewed and edited the content as needed, and I take full responsibility for the content of the thesis.

All writing in this thesis is original. The complete \texttt{git} commit history documents the progress of the thesis and can be used to verify that it was written by a human.
